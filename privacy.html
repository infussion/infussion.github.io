Privacy policy: Vinci (personal)

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Vinci is a private personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's data is collected.

What Vinci accesses

With the owner's permission, Vinci reads the owner's Google Calendar list and events (title, times, location, and description; never attendees) and, only after the owner approves each change, creates, changes, or deletes events on calendars the owner owns. It sends no invitations or emails.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show the owner their calendar and to answer the owner's questions. To answer, relevant event details are sent to OpenAI's API, which processes them on Vinci's behalf; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models and may keep it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.

Storage and sharing

Google access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. Calendar data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Vinci's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting Google Calendar in Vinci, which revokes Vinci's access and deletes the stored tokens, or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
